After the open house

Turn a phone photo into a saved PDF.

Inside Shelene Access, open 🗂️ 🎶 Docs & Media 🎶 🗂️. Use the Sign-in sheet → PDF area to save the paper sheet without needing a scanner.

  1. Tap Take or upload photo.
  2. On iPhone, choose Take Photo and photograph the completed sign-in sheet.
  3. Wait while the photo is converted to a PDF.
  4. Confirm the saved filename in the private file list, then download or open it when needed.

Important: Docs & Media currently stores files in the browser on that same device. Use the same phone/browser to retrieve them, or download the PDF and place a master copy in Drive, Dropbox, or your brokerage system.

Saved filename

Property address + date + time.

The capture workflow automatically names the PDF using the property address and the moment it was created.

12518_W_Sunset_Boulevard_[Day-Date]_[Time].pdf

Example: 12518_W_Sunset_Boulevard_Saturday-Aug-15-2026_7-28-PM.pdf

Open-house checklist

The simple sequence.

Before guests arrive

Open Shelene Access, verify the printable sign and sign-in sheet, and make sure your phone is ready to photograph the completed sheet later.

During the event

Direct guests to the RSVP page or sign-in sheet. Offer Shelene’s digital card so people can save her contact details immediately.

After the event

Photograph the sign-in sheet, save the generated PDF in Docs & Media, review the RSVP Desk, and begin personal follow-up.
